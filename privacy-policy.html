Privacy Policy for Punch Clock Pro

Effective Date: 05/01/2025

Magnus Tempus ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Punch Clock Pro mobile application ("the App") and is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information in connection with the App.

Information We Collect

Punch Clock Pro collects and processes the following information:

  • Employee names
  • Time stamps associated with punch events
  • Punch types (e.g., in, out)

This information is necessary to provide accurate time and attendance tracking services.

How We Use Information

The collected information is used solely for the purpose of recording employee time punches and making that information available to your employer through the Attendance on Demand system. We do not use this information for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Storage

Punch Clock Pro stores punch transaction data securely. We do not currently store transfer data or other personal information beyond what is required for punch tracking. Future updates may include transfer functionality, and this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Sharing of Information

We do not share or sell any personal information to third parties. Data is transmitted securely to your employer’s Attendance on Demand environment and is managed according to their internal policies.

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Your Rights

Employees should contact their employer for questions or requests regarding data management and correction, as Magnus Tempus acts as a data processor on behalf of your employer.
